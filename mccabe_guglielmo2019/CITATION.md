# How to cite these data

If you use `Frontiers_BMR_data.csv` please cite both the paper and the dataset.

## Paper

McCabe, B. J., & Guglielmo, C. G. (2019). Migration Takes Extra Guts for Juvenile Songbirds: Energetics and Digestive Physiology During the First Journey. Frontiers in Ecology and Evolution, 7. https://doi.org/10.3389/fevo.2019.00381

Link: <https://doi.org/10.3389/fevo.2019.00381>

## Dataset (Dryad)

Guglielmo, C., & McCabe, B. (2019). *Migration takes extra guts for juvenile songbirds: energetics and digestive physiology during the first journey* (Version 1) [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/DRYAD.P5HQBZKKH

Link: <https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.p5hqbzkkh>
