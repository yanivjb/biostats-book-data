# How to cite these data

If you use `Didikoglu_2023_project.csv`, cite the paper and the original data source below.

## Paper

Didikoglu, A., Mohammadian, N., Johnson, S., van Tongeren, M., Wright, P., Casson, A. J., Brown, T. M., & Lucas, R. J. (2023). Associations between light exposure and sleep timing and sleepiness while awake in a sample of UK adults in everyday life. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 120(42), e2301608120. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2301608120

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2301608120>

## Original data

Didikoglu, Altug, Lucas, Robert J, & Brown, Timothy M (2023). *Data: Associations between light exposure and sleep timing and sleepiness while awake in a sample of UK adults in everyday life* [Dataset]. University of Manchester. https://doi.org/10.48420/23786238

Source: <https://doi.org/10.48420/23786238>

Source table used: `Didikoglu_et_al_2023_PNAS_baseline.csv`.

Original file/worksheet: `23786238/Didikoglu_et_al_2023_PNAS_baseline.csv`.

## Teaching version

This folder contains a classroom subset or summary of the original data. The README describes the selection, aggregation, transformations and missing-value handling. The teaching CSV is not the complete original study dataset. No paper PDFs, raw sequencing files or instructor solutions are included.
