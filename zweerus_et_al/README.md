Title: Mating status affects female choice when females are signallers


The data set LATENCY reports the mating latencies of mated and virgin females. Male and female pupal mass were included in the analysis (see methods). The time from the start of the experiment to the moment of mating is reported as latency in minute (latency_min column).

Column names meaning:

Experiment_date	= date of experiment

Cup_id	= unique identity of the testing cup

female_status = female mating status

feamle_mass_grams = mass of female measured in grams

male_mass_grams = mass of male measured in grams

Grid_Location = location of the cup in the experimental set up (i.e., grid)

Mating	latency_min = mating latency measured in minutes
