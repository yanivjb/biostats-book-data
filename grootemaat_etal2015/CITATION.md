# How to cite these data

If you use `Grootemaat_2015_project.csv`, cite the paper and the original data source below.

## Paper

Grootemaat, S., Wright, I. J., van Bodegom, P. M., Cornelissen, J. H. C., & Cornwell, W. K. (2015). Burn or rot: leaf traits explain why flammability and decomposability are decoupled across species. Functional Ecology, 29(11), 1486-1497. https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.12449

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.12449>

## Original data

Grootemaat, Saskia, Wright, Ian J., van Bodegom, Peter M., Cornelissen, Johannes H. C., & Cornwell, William K. (2016). *Data from: Burn or rot: leaf traits explain why flammability and decomposability are decoupled across species* (Version 1) [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.m41f1

Source: <https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.m41f1>

Source table used: `MEANS.csv`.

Original file/worksheet: `Grootemaat+2015_FE_Dryad/MEANS.csv`.

## Teaching version

This folder contains a classroom subset or summary of the original data. The README describes the selection, aggregation, transformations and missing-value handling. The teaching CSV is not the complete original study dataset. No paper PDFs, raw sequencing files or instructor solutions are included.
