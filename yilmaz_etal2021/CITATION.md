# How to cite these data

If you use `isopod_thermal_tolerance.csv` please cite both the paper and the dataset.

## Paper

Yilmaz, A. R., Diamond, S. E., & Martin, R. A. (2020). Evidence for the evolution of thermal tolerance, but not desiccation tolerance, in response to hotter, drier city conditions in a cosmopolitan, terrestrial isopod. Evolutionary Applications, 14(1), 12–23. Portico. https://doi.org/10.1111/eva.13052

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1111/eva.13052>

## Dataset (Dryad)

Yilmaz, A. R., Diamond, S., & Martin, R. (2020). *Data from: Evidence for the evolution of thermal tolerance but not desiccation tolerance in response to hotter, drier city conditions in a cosmopolitan, terrestrial isopod* (Version 1) [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/DRYAD.R4XGXD297

Link: <https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.r4xgxd297>
