# How to cite these data

If you use `DRYAD_DATA.csv` please cite both the paper and the dataset.

## Paper

Videlier, M., Careau, V., Wilson, A. J., & Rundle, H. D. (2020). Quantifying selection on standard metabolic rate and body mass in
            *Drosophila melanogaster*. Evolution, 75(1), 130–140. Portico. https://doi.org/10.1111/evo.14126

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1111/evo.14126>

## Dataset (Dryad)

Rundle, H. D., Videlier, M., Careau, V., & Wilson, A. (2020). *Quantifying selection on standard metabolic rate and body mass in Drosophila melanogaster* (Version 3) [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/DRYAD.63XSJ3V17

Link: <https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.63xsj3v17>
