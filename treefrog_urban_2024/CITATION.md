# How to cite these data

If you use `data_envir.txt`, `data_urb_anu_cg.txt` please cite both the paper and the dataset.

## Paper

Provete, D. B., & Severgnini, M. R. (2024). The effect of urbanization and local environmental heterogeneity on phenotypic variability in a tropical frog. https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.11.28.625871

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.11.28.625871>

## Dataset (Dryad)

Severgnini, M., & Provete, D. (2024). *The effect of urbanisation and local environmental heterogeneity on phenotypic variability of a tropical treefrog* (Version 6) [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/DRYAD.3XSJ3TXP2

Link: <https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.3xsj3txp2>
