# How to cite these data

If you use `Functional_traits.csv` please cite both the paper and the dataset.

## Paper

Zhou, W., Tao, Y., Peng, L., Zheng, H., Zhou, X., Yin, B., Zhang, J., & Zhang, Y. (2024). Balancing the nutrient needs: Optimising growth in Malus sieversii seedlings through tailored nitrogen and phosphorus effects. Plant, Cell & Environment. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39188105/

## Dataset (Dryad)

Zhou, W., Ye, T., Peng, L., Zheng, H., Zhou, X., Yin, B., Zhang, J., & Zhang, Y. (2024). *Balancing the nutrient needs: Optimizing growth in Malus sieversii seedlings through tailored nitrogen and phosphorus effects* (Version 6) [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/DRYAD.BRV15DVJ0
