# How to cite these data

If you use `data_defense_traits_Leaf_Data.csv` please cite both the paper and the dataset.

## Paper

Yan, K., Xia, W., Yu, K., Yang, X., Wang, X., Wang, J., Zhou, Y., Jing, X., Zhang, H., Jiang, Y., Han, X., & Yu, L. (2025). Grazing disrupts the trade‐off between silica‐ and phenol‐based plant defences along an aridity gradient in grasslands. Functional Ecology, 39(12), 3474-3486. https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.70206

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.70206>

## Dataset (Dryad)

Yu, L., Yan, K., Xia, W., Yu, K., Yang, X., Wang, X., Wang, J., Zhou, Y., Jing, X., Zhang, H., Jiang, Y., & Han, X. (2025). *Plant defense trait responses to grazing along an aridity gradient in Inner Mongolian grasslands* (Version 3) [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/DRYAD.31ZCRJF1D

Link: <https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.31zcrjf1d>
