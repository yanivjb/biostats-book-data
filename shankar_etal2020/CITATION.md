# How to cite these data

If you use `Shankar_2020_project.csv`, cite the paper and the original data source below.

## Paper

Shankar, A., Schroeder, R. J., Wethington, S. M., Graham, C. H., & Powers, D. R. (2020). Hummingbird torpor in context: duration, more than temperature, is the key to nighttime energy savings. Journal of Avian Biology, 51(5), jav.02305. https://doi.org/10.1111/jav.02305

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1111/jav.02305>

## Original data

Shankar, Anusha, Schroeder, Rebecca J., Wethington, Susan M., Graham, Catherine H., & Powers, Donald R. (2020). *Hummingbird torpor in context: duration, more than temperature, is the key to nighttime energy savings* (Version 1) [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.t4b8gthzh

Source: <https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.t4b8gthzh>

## Teaching version

This folder contains a classroom subset or summary of the original data. The README describes the selection, aggregation, transformations and missing-value handling. The teaching CSV is not the complete original study dataset. No paper PDFs, raw sequencing files or instructor solutions are included.
