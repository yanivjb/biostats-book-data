Summary of Dryad Submission For:

Hund et al. 2022 “Population-Level Variation in Parasite Resistance Due to Differences in Immune Initiation and Rate of Response.”

Evolution Letters

Filename: 2018_Field_Data.csv

Description: A .csv file containing data from uninfected and infected fish collected from Gosling and Roselle Lakes in spring of 2018. Including visual fibrosis scores and the number and mass of tapeworms from each fish. Our original scoring system for fibrosis was 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 2+ (or 2.5) and this was later shifted to an ordinal scale for analysis (0, 1, 2, 3, 4). There is also information about the presence of encysted tapeworms. This dataset includes 31 uninfected and 31 infected fish from Gosling Lake and 30 uninfected and 32 infected fish from Roselle Lake. Please note that we weighed all tapeworms found within a fish together and our field scale only measured mass to 0.01g, so tapeworms weighing less then this were marked as 0.009g for analysis.
