---

This file explains all of the variables in each of the datasets that accompany: Jennette, MA, Snodgrass, JW, Forester, DC. 2018ms. Variation in age, body size, and reproductive traits among urban and rural amphibian populations.

**femalestoadage.csv**
* POP = Population of origin for female American toads; 2 = Urban C , 3 = Urban B, 4 = Urban A, 5= Suburban A, 6 = Rural
* Weight = somatic dry weight
* SVL = south vent length in mm
* Age = estimated age of American toads based on skeletal chronology
