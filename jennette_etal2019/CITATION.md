# How to cite these data

If you use `femalestoadage.csv` please cite both the paper and the dataset.

## Paper

Jennette, M. A., Snodgrass, J. W., & Forester, D. C. (2018). Variation in age, body size, and reproductive traits among urban and rural amphibian populations. Urban Ecosystems, 22(1), 137–147. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11252-018-0801-7

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1007/s11252-018-0801-7>

## Dataset (Dryad)

Jennette, M. A., Snodgrass, J. W., & Forester, D. C. (2019). *Data from: Variation in age, body size, and reproductive traits among urban and rural amphibian populations* (Version 1) [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/DRYAD.8P165G9

Link: <https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.8p165g9>
