# How to cite these data

If you use `Rittelmann-Woods_2023_Daucus_carota_48pots.csv`, cite the paper and the original data source below.

## Paper

Rittelmann-Woods, E., Lachaise, T., & van Kleunen, M. (2023). Negative effects of EPDM microplastic and cork granules on plant growth are mitigated by earthworms and likely caused by their structural properties. Science of The Total Environment, 897, 165354. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scitotenv.2023.165354

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scitotenv.2023.165354>

## Original data

van Kleunen, Mark, Rittelmann-Woods, Elina, & Lachaise, Tom (2024). *EPDM microplastic and earthworm effects on plants* (Version 6) [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.xgxd254n7

Source: <https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.xgxd254n7>

Source table used: `Rittelmann-WoodsEtAl_2023_MPEffectsOnPlants_Data.csv`.

Original file/worksheet: `doi_10_5061_dryad_xgxd254n7__v20240111/Rittelmann-WoodsEtAl_2023_MPEffectsOnPlants_Data.txt`.

The teaching CSV is not the complete original study dataset. No paper PDFs, raw sequencing files or instructor solutions are included.
