# How to cite these data

If you use `Westley_2024_project.csv`, cite the paper and the original data source below.

## Paper

Westley, J., García, F. C., Warfield, R., & Yvon-Durocher, G. (2024). The community background alters the evolution of thermal performance. Evolution Letters, 8(4), 505-513. https://doi.org/10.1093/evlett/qrae007

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1093/evlett/qrae007>

This folder contains a classroom subset or summary of the original data. The README describes the selection, aggregation, transformations and missing-value handling. The teaching CSV is not the complete original study dataset. No paper PDFs, raw sequencing files or instructor solutions are included.
