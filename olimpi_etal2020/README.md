# Exclosures_BerryDamage.csv

**Paper:** Olimpi et al. 2020. Shifts in species interactions and farming contexts mediate net effects of birds in agroecosystems. Ecological Applications  
https://doi.org/10.1002/eap.2115

**Data:** https://doi.org/10.25338/B8TK62

This folder contains only `Exclosures_BerryDamage.csv` from the original deposit (downloaded 2026-11-06, unmodified). Please cite both the paper and the dataset.

_Below: the authors' README (Exclosures_BerryDamage.txt), NOT trimmed automatically - edit by hand_

---

date- date berries were harvested
season- early or late; early (April/May) or late (July) season exclosure experiment
site- unique farm code
treatment- E or C; exclosure (E) or control (C) treatment
excl_num- 1, 2, or 3; exclosure number for paired treatment plots
num_berries- number of berries harvested
leaf_roller- number of berries with leaf-roller damage
snail_slug- number of berries with snail or slug damage
other_invert- number of berries with other invertebrate damage
lyg_poly1- number of berries with <10% "cat-facing" berry malformation due to Lygus spp. or under-pollination
lyg_poly2- number of berries with 10-25% "cat-facing" berry malformation due to Lygus spp. or under-pollination
lyg_poly3- number of berries with >25% "cat-facing" berry malformation due to Lygus spp. or under-pollination
bird_flesh - number of berries with bird damage to berry flesh
bird_seed- number of berries where birds removed seeds
bird_feces- number of berries with bird fecal contamination
vert- number of berries with vertebrate damage that could not be confirmed as being from birds
economic_damage- number of berries with severe damage that would prevent berries from being sold; includes any vertebrate damage, bird fecal contamination, berries with >25% malformation due to Lygus spp. or under-pollination, and other damage attributed to slugs, snails, or leaf-rolling caterpillars
invert_damage_economic- number of berries with severe damage that would prevent berries from being sold; includes berries with >25% malformation due to Lygus spp. or under-pollination, and other damage attributed to slugs, snails, or leaf-rolling caterpillars
invert_damage- number of berries with any invertebrate damage
vert_total- number of berries with any bird or other vertebrate damage
variety- strawberry variety of plants within treatment plot
dist_noncrop_edge- distance (m) from exclosure to closest noncrop edge
fencing_wires- average length (m) of fencing and wires with a 50m radius sampling circle on the farm
bird_practices- the total number of bird deterrent practices used on the farm
localdiv- standardized index of local diversification practices used on the farm
cropdiv_500m- crop diversity within 500m radius of farm
Semi_250- proportion of seminatural habitat within a 1km radius, calculated using Gaussian decay rate parameter of 250
Semi_750- proportion of seminatural habitat within a 1km radius, calculated using Gaussian decay rate parameter of 750
Semi_1250- proportion of seminatural habitat within a 1km radius of exclosure, calculated using Gaussian decay rate parameter of 1250
Semi_1km- proportion of seminatural habitat within a 1km radius
