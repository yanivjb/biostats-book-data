# How to cite these data

If you use `Sohlstroem_et_al_Data_Dryad.csv` please cite both the paper and the dataset.

## Paper

Sohlström, E. H., Marian, L., Barnes, A. D., Haneda, N. F., Scheu, S., Rall, B. C., Brose, U., & Jochum, M. (2018). Applying generalized allometric regressions to predict live body mass of tropical and temperate arthropods. Ecology and Evolution, 8(24), 12737–12749. Portico. https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.4702

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.4702>
