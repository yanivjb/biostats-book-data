# How to cite these data

If you use `Kimura_2015_project.csv`, cite the paper and the original data source below.

## Paper

Kimura, K., & Chiba, S. (2015). The direct cost of traumatic secretion transfer in hermaphroditic land snails: individuals stabbed with a love dart decrease lifetime fecundity. Proceedings of the Royal Society B: Biological Sciences, 282(1804), 20143063. https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2014.3063

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2014.3063>

## Original data

Kimura, Kazuki, & Chiba, Satoshi (2015). *Data from: The direct cost of traumatic secretion transfer in hermaphroditic land snails: individuals stabbed with a love dart decrease lifetime fecundity* (Version 1) [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.qg258

Source: <https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.qg258>

## Teaching version

This folder contains a classroom subset or summary of the original data. The README describes the selection, aggregation, transformations and missing-value handling. The teaching CSV is not the complete original study dataset. No paper PDFs, raw sequencing files or instructor solutions are included.
