# How to cite these data

If you use `Simon_2023_project.csv`, cite the paper and the original data source below.

## Paper

Simon, R., Dreissen, A., Leroy, H., Berg, M. P., & Halfwerk, W. (2023). Acoustic camouflage increases with body size and changes with bat echolocation frequency range in a community of nocturnally active Lepidoptera. Journal of Animal Ecology, 92(12), 2363-2372. https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2656.14016

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2656.14016>

## Original data

Simon, Ralph, Dreissen, Alrike, Leroy, Helene, Berg, Matty, & Halfwerk, Wouter (2023). *Acoustic camouflage increases with body size and bat echolocation frequency range in a community of nocturnally-active Lepidoptera* (Version 4) [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.66t1g1k73

Source: <https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.66t1g1k73>

Source table used: `Data_for_dryad__fully_descaled.csv`.

Original file/worksheet: `doi_10_5061_dryad_66t1g1k73__v20231012/Data_for_dryad.xlsx | worksheet fully descaled`.

## Teaching version

This folder contains a classroom subset or summary of the original data. The README describes the selection, aggregation, transformations and missing-value handling. The teaching CSV is not the complete original study dataset. No paper PDFs, raw sequencing files or instructor solutions are included.
