# How to cite these data

If you use `Fong_2019_project.csv`, cite the paper and the original data source below.

## Paper

Fong, S., Buechel, S. D., Boussard, A., Kotrschal, A., & Kolm, N. (2019). Plastic changes in brain morphology in relation to learning and environmental enrichment in the guppy (Poecilia reticulata). Journal of Experimental Biology, , jeb.200402. https://doi.org/10.1242/jeb.200402

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1242/jeb.200402>

## Original data

Fong, Stephanie, Buechel, Severine Denise, Boussard, Annika, Kotrschal, Alexander, & Kolm, Niclas (2019). *Data from: Plastic changes in brain morphology in relation to learning and environmental enrichment in the guppy (Poecilia reticulata)* (Version 1) [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.c4q7h62

Source: <https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.c4q7h62>

Source table used: `Fong_et_al_brain_plasticity_data__Data.csv`.

Original file/worksheet: `Fong_et_al_brain_plasticity_data.xlsx | worksheet Data`.

## Teaching version

This folder contains a classroom subset or summary of the original data. The README describes the selection, aggregation, transformations and missing-value handling. The teaching CSV is not the complete original study dataset. No paper PDFs, raw sequencing files or instructor solutions are included.
