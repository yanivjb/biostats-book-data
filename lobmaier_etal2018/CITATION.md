# How to cite these data

If you use `Lobmaier_2018_project.csv`, cite the paper and the original data source below.

## Paper

Lobmaier, J. S., Fischbacher, U., Wirthmüller, U., & Knoch, D. (2018). The scent of attractiveness: levels of reproductive hormones explain individual differences in women's body odour. Proceedings of the Royal Society B: Biological Sciences, 285(1886), 20181520. https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2018.1520

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2018.1520>

## Original data

Lobmaier, Janek, Fischbacher, Urs, Wirthmüller, Urs, Knoch, Daria, & Lobmaier, Janek S. (2018). *Data from: The scent of attractiveness: levels of reproductive hormones explain individual differences in women’s body odour* (Version 1) [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.g5n1785

Source: <https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.g5n1785>

Source table used: `BodyOdour_Homrones_Data.csv`.

Original file/worksheet: `Lobmaier_Data/BodyOdour_Homrones_Data.csv`.

## Teaching version

This folder contains a classroom subset or summary of the original data. The README describes the selection, aggregation, transformations and missing-value handling. The teaching CSV is not the complete original study dataset. No paper PDFs, raw sequencing files or instructor solutions are included.
