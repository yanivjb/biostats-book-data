# How to cite these data

If you use `pupa.csv` please cite both the paper and the dataset.

## Paper

Baranov, V., Losana‐García, J., Pascual‐Gil, J., Bermejo, S. F., & Hamerlik, L. (2025). Impact of temperature and hypoxia on the size and survival of aquatic insects. Ecological Entomology, 50(6), 1023–1032. Portico. https://doi.org/10.1111/een.13469

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1111/een.13469>

## Dataset (Dryad)

Baranov, V., Losana-García, J., Pascual, J., Fernández Bermejo, S., & Hamerlik, L. (2025). *Impact of temperature and hypoxia on the size and survival of aquatic insects* (Version 6) [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/DRYAD.8PK0P2P0X

Link: <https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.8pk0p2p0x>
