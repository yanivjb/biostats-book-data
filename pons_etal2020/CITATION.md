# How to cite these data

If you use `Pons_2020_project.csv`, cite the paper and the original data source below.

## Paper

Pons, C., Voß, A., Schweiger, R., & Müller, C. (2020). Effects of drought and mycorrhiza on wheat and aphid infestation. Ecology and Evolution, 10(19), 10481-10491. https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.6703

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.6703>

## Original data

Pons, Caroline, Voss, Ann-Cathrin, Schweiger, Rabea, & Müller, Caroline (2021). *Effects of drought and mycorrhiza on wheat and aphid infestation* (Version 4) [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.05qfttf13

Source: <https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.05qfttf13>

Source table used: `Biomass.csv`.

Original file/worksheet: `Data_Manuscript_Pons_et_al_Ecol_Evol/Biomass.csv`.

## Teaching version

This folder contains a classroom subset or summary of the original data. The README describes the selection, aggregation, transformations and missing-value handling. The teaching CSV is not the complete original study dataset. No paper PDFs, raw sequencing files or instructor solutions are included.
