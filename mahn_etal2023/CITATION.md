# How to cite these data

If you use `data_both_suborders.csv` please cite both the paper and the dataset.

## Paper

Mähn, L. A., Hof, C., Brandl, R., & Pinkert, S. (2023). Beyond latitude: Temperature, productivity and thermal niche conservatism drive global body size variation in Odonata. Global Ecology and Biogeography, 32(5), 656–667. Portico. https://doi.org/10.1111/geb.13661

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1111/geb.13661>

## Dataset (Dryad)

Mähn, L. A., Hof, C., Brandl, R., & Pinkert, S. (2023). *Data for: Beyond latitude: Temperature, productivity, and thermal niche conservatism drive global body size variation in Odonata* (Version 4) [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/DRYAD.HHMGQNKMG

Link: <https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.hhmgqnkmg>
