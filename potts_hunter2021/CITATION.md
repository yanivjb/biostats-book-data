# How to cite these data

If you use `Potts_2021_project.csv`, cite the paper and the original data source below.

## Paper

Potts, A. S., & Hunter, M. D. (2021). Unraveling the roles of genotype and environment in the expression of plant defense phenotypes. Ecology and Evolution, 11(13), 8542-8561. https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.7639

Link: <https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.7639>

## Original data

Potts, Abigail, & Hunter, Mark (2022). *Unraveling the roles of genotype and environment in the expression of plant defense phenotypes* (Version 2) [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.rv15dv47x

Source: <https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.rv15dv47x>

Source table used: `POTTS_2019_RT_all__sas.csv`.

Original file/worksheet: `doi_10_5061_dryad_rv15dv47x__v20220504/POTTS_2019_RT_all.xlsx | worksheet sas`.

## Teaching version

This folder contains a classroom subset or summary of the original data. The README describes the selection, aggregation, transformations and missing-value handling. The teaching CSV is not the complete original study dataset. No paper PDFs, raw sequencing files or instructor solutions are included.
